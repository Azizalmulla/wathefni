## Who we are

The OctoHR Employee App ("the app") is provided by OctoHR ("we", "us") to let
employees of companies that use OctoHR receive notifications and complete simple
HR self-service tasks.

Your employer is the **data controller** of your employment data. OctoHR acts as
a **data processor** on the employer's behalf. Questions about your data should
be directed to your employer's HR team; this policy explains what the app itself
collects and how it is handled.

## What the app collects

- **Account & identity**: your phone number and name, used to sign you in and link
  you to your employer's workspace.
- **Employment data you view in the app**: onboarding checklist items, shifts,
  attendance status, leave requests and balances, and documents you have
  submitted. This data originates from your employer's HR records.
- **Documents you upload**: files you choose to attach (e.g. an ID or certificate)
  for HR review.
- **Push notification token**: a device push token (via Apple/Google push
  services) so we can send you reminders. Tied to your account and device.
- **Device/app basics**: platform (iOS/Android) and app version, for delivery and
  support.

The app does **not** collect location, contacts, microphone, or advertising
identifiers, and contains **no third-party analytics or advertising trackers**.

## How we use it

- To authenticate you and show you your own employment information.
- To deliver notifications and reminders you would otherwise receive by other
  channels.
- To let you submit leave requests and documents to your HR team.

We do **not** sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising. Data
is shared only with: your employer (the controller), and the minimum required
service providers that operate the platform (hosting, email, and the push
notification relay).

## Permissions

The app requests permissions only when you use the related feature:

- **Notifications** — to deliver reminders. The app is fully usable without it
  (an in-app inbox always shows your notifications).
- **Camera / Photos** — only when you choose to capture or attach a document.

## Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your employment relationship with the
company using OctoHR requires, per your employer's policies and applicable law.

## Your choices

- Turn push notifications on/off in Settings (or in your device settings).
- Request account deletion from within the app (Settings → Request account
  deletion). Because your employer is the data controller, this sends a request
  to your HR team to handle in line with their recordkeeping obligations.
- Sign out at any time.

## Security

Session credentials are stored in the device's secure keystore (iOS Keychain /
Android Keystore). Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is scoped so
you can only ever see your own records.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact your employer's HR team or OctoHR support through
the support destination linked from the app.
