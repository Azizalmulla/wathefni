OctoHR employee account

Delete your account and data

You can initiate an account and personal-data deletion request here without opening the OctoHR app. Your request is sent to the HR team that controls your company workspace.

Submit a deletion request

Use the company code and work email address or phone number registered with your OctoHR Employee account. To protect account privacy, the confirmation does not reveal whether an account exists.

What happens next

  1. If the details match an OctoHR Employee account, one idempotent deletion task is created for the correct company's HR team.
  2. Your employer verifies the request and determines which employment records must be retained. Submitting this form does not immediately erase statutory records.
  3. When the request is approved and completed, account access is deactivated and data that is not subject to a retention requirement is deleted or irreversibly anonymized. HR will provide the applicable verification steps, expected completion timing, and retention outcome.

Data deleted or deactivated

When your employer completes an approved request, the requested deletion scope includes revoking or deleting OctoHR access sessions, refresh credentials, push-notification tokens, and app-device access. Profile/contact data, uploaded copies, support data, and other personal data are deleted or irreversibly anonymized when they are no longer required for employment administration, security, legal obligations, or a live dispute.

Data that may be retained

Your employer is the data controller. It may need to retain core employment records such as contracts and personnel-file documents, payroll and payslip records, attendance and leave history, approvals, and security/audit evidence.

For Kuwait private-sector workspaces, OctoHR's configured baseline prevents automatic purging of governed personnel records for at least 365 days after employment ends. A longer period applies when required by the employer's documented retention policy, payroll or tax obligations, an investigation, a legal hold, or an active claim. Records under a legal hold are kept until the hold or claim is resolved and the applicable retention period has ended. Your HR team can provide the exact policy that applies to your employment record.
