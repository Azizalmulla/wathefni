> **Video Type:** Product Demo
> **Estimated Duration:** 3–4 minutes
> **Date:** February 20, 2026

## SCENE 1: THE PROBLEM (0:00 – 0:30)

**[ON SCREEN]** Montage: stacks of printed CVs, overwhelmed HR person scrolling through emails, WhatsApp groups flooded with "any jobs?" messages, a frustrated manager at a desk.

**NARRATOR (VO):**

>

>
> What if there was a smarter way?

**[ON SCREEN]** Screen fades to black. AI Octopus logo appears.

**NARRATOR (VO):**
> Introducing **AI Recruiter** — by AI Octopus.

---

## SCENE 2: THE SOLUTION — 10-SECOND PITCH (0:30 – 0:45)

**[ON SCREEN]** Clean product graphic: QR code → WhatsApp icon → AI brain → Dashboard.

**NARRATOR (VO):**
> Candidates scan a QR code. Send their CV on WhatsApp. Our AI reads it, screens them, and delivers ranked candidates straight to your phone — in minutes, not days.
>
> No app to download. No portal to log into. Just WhatsApp.

---

## SCENE 3: CANDIDATE FLOW — LIVE DEMO (0:45 – 2:00)

**[ON SCREEN]** Phone screen recording — candidate's perspective.

### Step 1 — QR Code

**NARRATOR (VO):**
> Let's see it in action. Say your company is hiring a Python Developer.

**[ON SCREEN]** Show a printed job poster with a QR code on it. Camera zooms into the QR code. Phone scans it.

**NARRATOR (VO):**
> The candidate scans the QR code on your job post — could be on Instagram, a career fair banner, or your office wall.

**[ON SCREEN]** WhatsApp opens. Pre-filled message reads: `APPLY-AIOCTOPUS-PYDEV`. Candidate taps send.

### Step 2 — AI Greets

**[ON SCREEN]** WhatsApp chat. AI responds:

> **AI Recruiter:** Hi! 👋 You're applying for **Python Developer** at **AI Octopus**. Great choice! Please send me your CV as a PDF and I'll get you started.

**NARRATOR (VO):**
> The AI instantly knows which company and which role. No confusion, no manual routing.

### Step 3 — CV Upload

**[ON SCREEN]** Candidate sends a PDF file in WhatsApp.

**NARRATOR (VO):**
> The candidate sends their CV — just like sending a photo to a friend.

**[ON SCREEN]** AI responds:

> **AI Recruiter:** Got it! ✅ I've read your CV. You're **Ahmed Ali** — BSc in Computer Science from Kuwait University, 3 years of experience with Python and Django. Looks solid!
>
> I have a few quick questions to complete your application.

**NARRATOR (VO):**
> Our AI reads the entire CV in seconds. Extracts name, education, experience, skills — everything. No manual data entry.

### Step 4 — Screening Questions

**[ON SCREEN]** WhatsApp conversation continues:

> **AI Recruiter:** What's your current visa/residency status in Kuwait?
>
> **Candidate:** I have a valid transferable work permit.
>
> **AI Recruiter:** What's your expected monthly salary in KD?
>
> **Candidate:** Around 800 KD.
>
> **AI Recruiter:** When can you start?
>
> **Candidate:** Immediately.
>
> **AI Recruiter:** How many years of experience do you have with Python?
>
> **Candidate:** 3 years, mostly Django and FastAPI.

**NARRATOR (VO):**
> The AI asks your custom screening questions — visa status, salary, availability, plus whatever's specific to the role. All in a natural conversation, in English or Arabic.

### Step 5 — Done

**[ON SCREEN]** AI responds:

> **AI Recruiter:** Your application is complete! ✅ The HR team will review your profile and get back to you. Good luck! 🤞

**NARRATOR (VO):**
> That's it. Two minutes. The candidate applied, got screened, and their data is already in your dashboard. No forms, no emails, no waiting.
