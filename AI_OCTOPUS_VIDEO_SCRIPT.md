## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Pace:** Fast. Every shot is 2-4 seconds max. Match cuts to beat drops.
- **Screen recordings:** WhatsApp convos (customer service + payment + recruiter). Film on a real phone.
- **Text overlays:** Big, bold, minimal words. Think Instagram Reel style.
- **Music:** Upbeat electronic, no lyrics. Artlist/Epidemic Sound — search "tech reel" or "startup energy."
- **Formats:** 9:16 vertical (Reels/TikTok primary), 16:9 horizontal cut for YouTube/LinkedIn.
- **Arabic version:** Same edit, swap voiceover + text overlays to Arabic. Keep product names in English.
