## Questionnaire (all partners)

1. Which attendance/access brands and models do you install most in Kuwait (top 3)?
2. What software do customers typically run (BioTime version, HikCentral, BioStar, IBA, CrossChex, none/USB export)?
3. Do customers usually: live sync to HR, nightly Excel/USB, or both?
4. Which APIs/SDKs can you enable for a third-party HRMS (docs, license SKUs, sample)?
5. Can integration be limited to **event logs only** (no fingerprint/face templates or images)?
6. Typical network layout: devices → local server only, or devices with internet ADMS/cloud?
7. Would you partner with Wathefni for install, mapping, and L1 support? Commercial model?
8. Lead time and sandbox/demo hardware availability in Kuwait?
