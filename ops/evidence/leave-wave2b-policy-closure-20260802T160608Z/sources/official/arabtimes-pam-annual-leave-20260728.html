Here's What Kuwait's Public Authority of Manpower Says About Employees' Annual Leave Rights

Here's What Kuwait's Public Authority of Manpower Says About Employees' Annual Leave Rights

28/07/2026

Share:

KUWAIT CITY, Jul 28 : The Public Authority for Manpower (PAM) has reminded employees and employers of their rights and obligations regarding annual leave under Kuwait’s Labour Law, stressing that annual leave is a legal entitlement designed to protect both workers and employers.

In an advisory issued under its “Know Your Rights” campaign, PAM stated that workers in the private sector are entitled to at least 30 working days of paid annual leave after completing the required period of service.

According to Article 70 of the Labour Law, an employee/worker is not entitled to annual leave during the first year of employment until completing at least six months of service with the same employer.

The authority also clarified that employers have the right to schedule annual leave based on workplace requirements, in as per Article 72 of the Labour Law. Employers may divide an employee’s annual leave period, provided it is done with the worker’s consent and in line with legal provisions.

PAM highlighted that employees must receive their annual leave payment before starting their leave, as stipulated under Article 71 of the Labour Law.

The authority urged both employers and employees to understand their legal rights and responsibilities, emphasizing that proper regulation of annual leave helps maintain a balance between business needs and workers’ welfare.

“Annual leave is a legal right,” PAM said, adding that its regulation serves the interests of both workers and employers.
